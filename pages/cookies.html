Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences, track your activity, and improve your browsing experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

  • corriesells_session: Maintains your login session
  • corriesells_cart: Stores your shopping cart items
  • corriesells_user: Remembers your user preferences

Performance & Analytics Cookies

  • _ga, _gid: Google Analytics - tracks site usage and user behavior
  • _gat: Google Analytics - limits request rate

Functional Cookies

  • corriesells_preferences: Stores your language and display preferences
  • corriesells_wishlist: Saves your wishlist items

Marketing & Targeting Cookies

  • Facebook Pixel: Tracks conversions for remarketing campaigns
  • Google Ads: Enables personalized product recommendations

3. Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for up to 2 years

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and site permissions

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect website functionality including:

  • Shopping cart may not persist
  • You may not stay logged in
  • Personalized recommendations won't work
  • Site performance analytics won't be collected

6. Third-Party Cookies

We use cookies from third-party services for analytics and marketing:

  • Google Analytics: Google Privacy Policy
  • Facebook: Facebook Privacy Policy
  • Stripe: Stripe Privacy Policy

7. Your Consent

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or contacting us.
