How to Measure

Chest/Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest while wearing a relaxed fit shirt. Keep the measuring tape parallel to the ground and comfortably snug (not tight).

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline (typically at the belly button level). Keep the tape relaxed but not loose - you should be able to fit one finger underneath.

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips/thighs. Stand with feet together and keep the tape parallel to the ground.

Sleeve Length (Men)

Measure from the center back neck, across the shoulder, and down to your wrist. Bend your arm slightly for accuracy.

Fit Guide

CORRIESELLS Fit Philosophy

  • Relaxed Fit: Loose throughout with room for movement. Ideal for comfort and layering.
  • Slim Fit: Tailored close to the body without being restrictive. Modern and sleek silhouette.
  • Oversized: Extra room through the body and arms. Statement piece and trendy look.
  • Fitted: Follows body contours naturally. Flattering and versatile for styling.

Note: All our garments are pre-shrunk for consistency. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort.
