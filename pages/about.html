About CORRIESELLS

CORRIESELLS is a premium clothing brand inspired by the natural elegance of cowrie shells. We believe in creating timeless, sustainable fashion that celebrates the ocean's beauty and our commitment to the environment.

Founded in 2021, CORRIESELLS combines sophisticated design with eco-conscious manufacturing. Every piece is crafted with premium, organic materials sourced ethically from around the world.

We are proudly based in Alabama, USA, serving customers across the United States and beyond.
