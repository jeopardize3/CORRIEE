    Shipping & Delivery

    Delivery timelines and tracking for every order.

    We offer domestic shipping for USA/Canada: Standard (5-7 business days, free on orders over $10,000), Express (2-3 business days, $15), and Overnight (next business day, $30).
    Yes, we ship to 150+ countries via DHL, FedEx, and UPS. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and weight, and duties or taxes may apply.
    All orders include tracking. You’ll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. Tracking is also available in your account dashboard.
    For international orders, customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes. These are determined by the destination country.

    Returns & Refunds

    Simple returns, clear timelines, no surprises.

    Returns are accepted within 30 days for items in original condition with tags attached. Return shipping is free for most countries.
    Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return. Your bank may take additional time to post the credit.

    Sizing & Fit

    Use the size guide and product reviews for fit advice.

    Check the size guide on each product page. We list measurements for chest, length, and more.
    Most items are true to size. For relaxed fits, size up if you’re between sizes.

    We accept credit and debit cards, PayPal, Google Pay, and Apple Pay. All transactions are encrypted.
