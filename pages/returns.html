Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: February 2026

30-Day Return Guarantee

We want you to be 100% satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your items, we offer a hassle-free 30-day return and refund policy.

Return Eligibility

  • Items must be in original, unworn condition
  • All tags must be attached
  • Items must be returned within 30 days of purchase
  • Original receipt or proof of purchase required

How to Return

  1. Contact our customer service at corriewhited@corriesells.com
  2. Receive a return authorization number
  3. Ship the item back using the prepaid label we provide
  4. We'll process your refund within 5-7 business days

Refund Timeline

  • Return Received: We'll confirm receipt within 2 business days
  • Processing: 5-7 business days after receipt
  • Refund Issued: To your original payment method

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? We offer free exchanges within 60 days of purchase.
